Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This explains what collects, why, who else sees it, and how you can control it. It is run by (). Questions: .

1. If you don't make an account

Your likes, playlists, recently played songs, name, theme, volume, equalizer and crossfade settings stay in your browser's local storage on your device. We do not receive them. You can erase them in Settings.

2. If you make an account

WhatWhy
Email address and password (the password is stored scrambled by our login service; we cannot read it)To sign you in
Display name, liked songs, playlists, recently playedTo sync them across your devices
Total listening time, last active time, account creation timeTo run the service and see how it is used
Confirmation that you are 18 or older and accepted these policies, and whenTo keep a record of consent
If you upload songs: audio file, cover image, title, artist name, language, genreTo review and play your songs in the app
If you report a song: the song and your reason (not your identity)To review copyright and safety complaints

We do not sell your data, show ads, use analytics, or record your screen.

3. Other services that see your visit

To work, the app talks to services that receive your IP address and browser details when the app asks them for something:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's files.
  • Supabase stores accounts, synced data and uploaded songs.
  • Jamendo, Audius and the Internet Archive supply songs and artwork shown in the app.
  • YouTube (Google), only if you open a result from "On YouTube". We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced player, but YouTube may still set cookies once you play a video.
  • Google Fonts, only if you allow it in the privacy prompt. If you decline, your device's own fonts are used.

Each of these has its own privacy policy.

4. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The app stores settings, your sign-in session and (without an account) your library in local storage, which is necessary for it to work. You can clear it in your browser or in Settings.

5. How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your synced data and your uploaded songs and files. Hosting providers may keep short-term server logs.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: Settings → Download my data.
  • Delete your account and data: Settings → Delete my account.
  • Change your mind about fonts: Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Correct or ask anything: email us. You can also complain to us first through our grievance officer, (), and to the data protection authority where you live (in India, the Data Protection Board).

7. Children

Accounts are for people 18 or older. The app can be used without an account, in which case we collect nothing about you.

8. Where data is stored

Account data is held by Supabase on servers that may be outside your country. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access rules so people can only read their own data, but no system is perfectly secure.

9. Changes

If this changes in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, for accounts, ask for your consent again if needed.
